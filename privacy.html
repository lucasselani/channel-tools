Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Channel Tools is a personal tool used only by its author on his own YouTube channels.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, it reads, and never changes, the channel's list of uploads (YouTube Data API, youtube.readonly) and the channel's statistics (YouTube Analytics API, yt-analytics.readonly).

How the data is used

The data is used only to write reports for the channel owner. It is stored on the owner's own computer and is not sent to any server, shared with anyone, sold or used for advertising.

Tokens

The OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Channel Tools' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
